# Moderation

Draft. The Square's sidebar states the same rules.

## What stays off the network

Do not post about a person who has not done the thing. A name, a household, or a rumour is not a subject. Write about events, places, and work.

Do not publish where someone lives. Do not publish coordinates, a street address, or a map pin for a member or a sensor.

## Who moderates

Moderators are members who have agreed to take a shift. The shift rotates. One person does not hold it as a permanent job. While the network is still one operator, that operator is the moderator, and the rotation starts when a second person is willing.

## Appeals

A removal or a warning can be appealed. The appeal goes to a moderator who did not make the original decision. The person appealing gets a short written reason either way.

Until there are two moderators, an appeal is a written note back to the operator, kept with the decision, and reviewed again when a second moderator exists.
